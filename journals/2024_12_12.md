## [[HomeLab]]
	- [[Reverse-Proxy]]
		- ### Enable an internal reverse-proxy
			- Login to PiHole and go to LOCAL DNS ==> CNAME RECORDS
				- And add a new record for the new subdomain:
					- Domain:  {}NewSubdomain}.rp.local
					  Target Domain: rp.local
		- ### Enable a new external reverse-proxy
			- Login to dyndns.org
				- In the list of current host names click on "Add Hostname".
